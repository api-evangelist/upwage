# Upwage

> Upwage is an AI interview platform that helps companies hire faster, fairer, and with lower turnover. Its AI interviewer screens candidates consistently and without bias, giving every applicant a fair shot while freeing recruiters from manual screening. Used by employers across retail, healthcare, and high-volume hiring with proven ROI.
